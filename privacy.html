Privacy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Data stored on your device

Guest data is stored in this browser’s local storage under the key “lassitude”. It includes your display name, preferences, habit schedules and notes, yes/no/skip logs, focus tasks and session history, garden history, reading-plan progress, private Scripture cards, prayer entries and requests, gratitude notes, goals and due dates, calendar events, workout routines and sessions, private workout journal entries, optional compressed photos, milestones, water logs, and prayer-time logs. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to access guest data.

If Supabase is configured and you create an account, account data is synced to the Supabase project only after you sign in; on first sign-in the app asks before uploading guest data. Supabase Auth handles passwords and confirmation/reset emails. Lassitude does not store passwords in local storage or its data tables. Row Level Security restricts synced rows to their owner. The project owner and Supabase still operate the account database under the project’s policies.

Use Export in Settings to download a JSON copy. Use Delete all data in Settings to clear Lassitude’s saved data from this browser. Clearing browser site data also removes it.

External services

Music playlists are embedded from Spotify only after you select a playlist. Spotify may receive your network and browser information under its own privacy policy. Lassitude does not include analytics, advertising or email subscriptions.

The Inter font is loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads, under Google’s privacy policy. No cookies are set for this.

NADIR coach and AI

For guests, NADIR runs entirely in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere. Your conversation is saved in this browser so it is there when you return; clear or export it from Settings → NADIR & chat.

If the site owner has enabled AI replies and you are signed in, your recent NADIR messages (up to the last 12) and a short summary of today’s progress (habits, workouts, focus minutes, devotional and water) are sent through the site’s Supabase server function to Anthropic’s Claude API to generate a reply. Your display name may be included so NADIR can address you; your email is not. Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms and does not use it to train models by default. Do not share sensitive health details in the chat.

If this static site is hosted by GitHub Pages or another host, that provider may process ordinary web request and security logs. Review the host’s privacy information for details.

Contact

Account features require a Supabase project configured by the site owner. For account-provider privacy practices, review Supabase’s privacy information and the project owner’s deployment policies.
